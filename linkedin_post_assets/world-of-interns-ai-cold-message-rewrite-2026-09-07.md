## Caption

Most AI-written cold messages fail for one simple reason:

they could be sent to anyone.

That is the problem.

If your message says, "I am very passionate and impressed by your profile," it sounds polite, but it gives the other person no reason to reply.

Use AI for structure, not for fake enthusiasm.

Try this prompt before you message a recruiter, alumnus, or hiring manager:

"Rewrite my draft in my voice. Keep it under 80 words. Include:
1. why I am reaching out to this person
2. one proof point from my work
3. one small ask
Remove praise I cannot prove. Do not invent experience."

Then run a final check:

- Could this message only go to this person?
- Is the ask easy to answer?
- Is every claim backed by something real?
- Would I still send it if AI had not written it?

AI can make a message cleaner.

Your job is to make it honest and specific.
