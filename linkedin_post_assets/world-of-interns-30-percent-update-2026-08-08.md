## Topic

Saturday Student Story: the 30 percent update interns can send before going too deep into a task, so managers can check direction early and prevent avoidable rework.

## Caption

Many interns wait until the work is 100 percent done before showing it.

That feels safe.

It often creates rework.

A better habit:

send a 30 percent update.

Keep it short.

1. This is the direction I am taking
2. This part is already working
3. This part is still uncertain
4. This is the decision I need checked

The point is not to ask your manager to do the work.

It is to make your thinking visible early enough to correct the path.

In most teams, a rough version at the right time is more useful than a polished version in the wrong direction.

If you are starting an internship, try this line:

"I have a rough direction ready. Could you check if this is the right shape before I go deeper?"

It shows ownership.

It also shows that you know feedback has a timing.
