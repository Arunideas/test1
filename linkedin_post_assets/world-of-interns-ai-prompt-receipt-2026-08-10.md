## Topic

Monday AI Tool: a simple "prompt receipt" habit for students using AI-assisted career drafts.

## Caption

Before you use AI for a resume bullet, email, or LinkedIn message, keep a small prompt receipt.

Most students only save the final answer.

Save three lines instead:

1. What you asked the AI to do.
2. The facts you gave it.
3. The final version you actually used.

This creates a quick audit trail.

If a recruiter asks about a project, you can return to the facts.
If a line sounds too polished, you can edit it back to your voice.
If AI added something you never did, you can catch it before sending.

AI can help you draft faster.
Your record helps you stay honest.

Try this today:
After your next AI-assisted career draft, paste the prompt and source facts under it before you send.

What is one career document where this habit would help you most?
