Caption:

Before you apply to an internship, spend 10 minutes checking fit.

Not 2 hours rewriting everything.

Not 10 seconds clicking easy apply.

Use this filter:

1. Circle the 3 skills the role repeats.

2. Match one project, course, task, or result to those skills.

3. Write one sentence that connects your proof to the role.

4. Decide: apply today, save for later, or skip.

This prevents two common mistakes:

Applying to everything with the same resume.

Waiting so long to personalize that the first shortlist is already full.

The goal is not to be perfect.

The goal is to be clearly relevant before the recruiter moves on.

If you cannot find even one honest proof point, skip that role and use the time on a better match.

What is your fastest way to decide if an internship is worth applying to?
