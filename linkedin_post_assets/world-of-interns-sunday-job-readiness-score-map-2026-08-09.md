## Caption

Sunday is a good day to read your assessment scores without panic.

A score is not a label.

It is a map.

This week, look at your latest test, mock interview, coding task, or resume review and sort the result into three buckets:

1. Strong signal
What can you already prove with a project, score, sample, or clear resume bullet?

2. Weak signal
Where do you only have a claim, not evidence?

3. Next practice
What is one small task you can do before the next application?

Most students see a low score and stop.

Better approach: ask what the score is pointing to.

If communication is weak, record a one-minute project explanation.

If technical depth is weak, write the README you avoided.

If role fit is weak, remove one application that does not match your proof.

One honest review can make next week's applications sharper.

What signal will you strengthen before Monday?
