## Topic

Saturday Student Story - the 5-line handover note habit.

## LinkedIn caption

Good student work becomes easier to trust when it can be handed over.

Most students finish a task and move on.

The problem is that interviews, resumes, and internships rarely ask, "Were you busy?"

They ask:

What did you own?

What changed because of your work?

Can someone else understand it without a meeting?

A simple handover note helps.

After any project, club task, freelance work, or internship assignment, write 5 lines:

1. What was the task?
2. What was unclear at the start?
3. What decision did you make?
4. Where is the proof saved?
5. What should the next person check first?

This is not extra documentation.

It is evidence.

It helps your manager trust the work.

It helps your future self explain the work.

It helps your resume sound specific instead of generic.

If you are building experience this week, save one handover note before you close the laptop.

What would you add to this note?
