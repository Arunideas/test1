# World of Interns LinkedIn Post - Students Who Get Callbacks

Some students get callbacks with average marks.

Not because their resume is flashy.

Because their application answers one simple question quickly:
"Can this person do the work we need?"

A pattern we see again and again:

1. They apply to fewer roles.
2. They match each project to the role.
3. They write outcomes, not responsibilities.
4. They keep one proof link ready.
5. They follow up once, politely.

This matters because recruiters are not reading with unlimited time.
They are scanning for fit, signal, and proof.

Before your next application, try this 10-minute check:

Pick one role.
Circle the 3 skills it repeats.
Move your closest project near the top.
Rewrite one bullet with a result.
Remove anything that does not support that role.

Average marks do not automatically close doors.
Unclear applications do.

What is one line on your resume that could show proof more clearly?
