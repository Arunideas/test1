```text
Before Monday applications begin, pause for 10 minutes and check one thing:

Does every role you are applying to have matching proof?

Make a simple table:

Role I want -> Proof I can show -> Proof still missing

For example:

Marketing intern -> one campaign idea, one analytics screenshot -> a short write-up of what I learned

Data intern -> one cleaned dataset, one chart -> a note explaining the decision I made

Content intern -> three published samples -> one example with performance or feedback

This is a small exercise, but it changes the quality of your applications.

It stops you from sending the same resume everywhere.
It shows you where your strongest evidence already is.
It gives you one practical gap to close this week.

You do not need a perfect portfolio before applying.

You do need enough visible proof that a recruiter can understand why this role fits you.

Tonight, choose one internship role and fill the three boxes.

Which proof gap can you close before sending the next application?
```
