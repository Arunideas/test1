## Caption

Most students save a rejection email as bad news.

Use it as a learning brief.

Not every rejection has a clear reason.
Many are automated.
Some are about timing, volume, or fit.

But you can still use AI to turn the moment into a better next application.

Try this prompt:

"I applied for this internship and did not get selected. Here is the role description and my resume/project summary. Do not guess the employer's reason. Build a learning backlog of 5 things I can improve before applying to similar roles."

Then ask AI to separate the list into:

1. Skills I can practise this week
2. Proof I need to add to my resume
3. Projects I should explain more clearly
4. Keywords I may understand only at surface level
5. Questions I should ask before applying again

This keeps the work honest.

AI should not invent why you were rejected.
It should help you decide what to improve next.

One rejection should not become a story about failure.

It can become a cleaner checklist for the next attempt.

If you reviewed your last rejection this way, what would be the first item in your learning backlog?
