## Post copy

Future skills are not only about new tools.

Sometimes the skill is simpler:

Can you explain why you chose one option over another?

Many internship updates sound like this:

"I made the report."
"I changed the design."
"I fixed the issue."

That tells the team what happened.
It does not show how you thought.

Try keeping a small decision log for important work:

1. What was the goal?
2. What options did you compare?
3. What constraint mattered most?
4. What did you choose?
5. When should it be checked again?

This helps in three places.

Your mentor can review your work faster.
Your handover becomes cleaner.
Your interview stories become more specific.

You do not need a long document.
Five clear lines are enough.

The future skill is not sounding confident after the work.
It is leaving a trace of your thinking while the work is happening.

What is one decision from this week that you should write down?
