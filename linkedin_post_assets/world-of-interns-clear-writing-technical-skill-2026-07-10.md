Topic: Friday Future Skill - why writing clearly is becoming a technical skill.

## Caption

Clear writing is becoming a technical skill.

Not because every student needs to write essays.

Because every modern job has moments where unclear writing breaks the work.

A prompt that is too vague gives a vague answer.
A bug report with no steps wastes engineering time.
A project note with no decision trail is hard to trust.
A resume bullet with no result gets skipped.

Students often treat writing as a separate "communication" subject.

At work, it sits inside the technical task.

If you can explain:

1. What problem you saw
2. What you tried
3. What changed
4. What still needs checking

you become easier to work with.

A simple practice:

After every small project, write a 5-line handover note.

- Goal
- Input
- Method
- Result
- Next risk

That note is proof of thinking.

It also helps in interviews because you are not trying to remember everything from scratch.

You can explain the work like someone who actually did it.

Which skill are you learning right now?
