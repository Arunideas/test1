## Caption

Your first useful feedback can feel personal.

It usually is not.

Maybe your manager says the report is too long.

Maybe a senior asks why you missed a detail.

Maybe someone says your update was unclear.

The defensive answer comes fast:

"But I worked hard on it."

That sentence may be true.

It may also close the conversation.

Try a calmer three-minute response:

1. Listen without solving immediately.
2. Repeat the exact change you heard.
3. Choose one action you will improve today.
4. Send a small update after you try it.

A simple reply can sound like this:

"Got it. I made the update too detailed. I will shorten the next one to the key decision, blocker, and next step. I will share that format tomorrow."

This does two things.

It shows you understood the feedback.

It shows you can turn it into behavior.

No intern is expected to know every workplace habit on day one.

But the interns people trust are often the ones who can hear correction without making the room heavier.

What is one feedback habit that helped you grow faster?
