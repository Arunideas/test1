## Caption

The students who will stand out in the AI era are not the ones who memorise the most prompts.

They are the ones who can debug their own thinking.

Before you accept an answer from AI, ask:

1. What assumption did I put into the question?
2. What evidence is the answer using?
3. What part still needs a human decision?
4. How would I explain this if someone challenged me?

This skill matters in internships because real work is rarely a clean textbook problem.

You will get vague tasks.
You will receive incomplete information.
You will need to decide what is useful, risky, missing, or worth checking again.

AI can help you move faster.

But your judgement decides whether the output is safe to use.

That judgement starts with noticing your own blind spots before someone else has to point them out.

Small practice for today:

Take one answer you got from AI this week and write three lines:

- What it got right
- What it assumed
- What you still verified yourself

That is not extra work.

That is career preparation.
