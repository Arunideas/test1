## LinkedIn caption

AI can help with resume bullets.

But it should not invent your internship story.

A better use:

Take your rough line first.

Example:
"Made a website for college event."

Then ask AI to rewrite it with only what you can prove:

1. What did you build?
2. Who used it?
3. What changed after it went live?
4. Which tool or skill matters for the role?

The final bullet should still sound like you.

Not like a template.
Not like fake impact.
Not like every number was added because AI suggested it.

Try this prompt:

"Rewrite this resume bullet for an internship application. Keep it honest. Do not add numbers I have not given. Make it clear what I built, what tools I used, and why it mattered."

AI is useful when it cleans the sentence.

It is risky when it creates the evidence.

What would you add to this check?
