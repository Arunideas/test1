## Caption

If you are stuck in the first week of an internship, ask earlier than feels comfortable.

Most students wait because they think asking makes them look weak.

The opposite usually happens.

A quiet intern who stays blocked for two days creates more uncertainty than an intern who says:

"I tried A, checked B, and I am stuck at C."

That one sentence shows effort.

It gives your mentor context.

It makes the next answer faster.

A simple help request can look like this:

1. What I was trying to do
2. What I already tried
3. Where I am stuck
4. What decision I need help with

You do not need to ask every five minutes.

But you also do not need to prove your seriousness by struggling silently.

This week, if you get blocked for more than 30 minutes, write down your attempts and ask with context.

That is not dependence.

That is working like a teammate.

What is one question you wish interns asked earlier?
