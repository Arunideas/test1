## Topic

Saturday Student Story - the first-week question log habit.

## LinkedIn caption

Your first week at work should leave a question log, not just a task list.

Many students try to look confident by staying quiet.

That feels safe for a day.

Then the same doubts come back during the task, the review, or the interview story later.

A better habit is simple:

Write down the questions that helped you understand the work.

Not every doubt.

Not every small confusion.

Only the questions that changed what you did next.

Try this 4-line log after each new task:

1. What did I not understand at first?
2. Who or what clarified it?
3. What changed in my work after that?
4. What will I ask earlier next time?

This does two useful things.

It shows you where you are actually learning.

It also gives you real interview material, because you can explain how you handled uncertainty instead of pretending it was never there.

Good interns do not know everything on day one.

They learn how to ask cleaner questions.

If you are starting a project, internship, or club role this month, keep a small question log for one week.

What is one question you wish you had asked earlier?
