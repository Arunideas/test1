## Caption

Your Sunday report does not need a dashboard.

It can be one question:

Which applications have proof behind them?

Many students make a list of internships first and look for evidence later.

That is why the same resume gets sent to roles that need different strengths.

Try a 15-minute evidence audit before Monday.

For every role on your shortlist, write one line:

1. Role fit: the skill this role is asking for
2. Resume proof: the project, assignment, internship, or result that shows it
3. Next edit: the one bullet or portfolio link that needs cleanup

If you cannot find proof yet, do not force the application.

Move it to a practice list. Build the proof. Come back with a better version.

If the proof is clear, apply with more care than speed.

A weekly report is useful only when it changes the next action.

Before this week starts, which saved internship has the strongest proof in your resume?
