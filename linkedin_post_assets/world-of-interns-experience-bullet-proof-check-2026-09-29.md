# World of Interns LinkedIn post - Experience bullet proof check

**Topic:** Turn experience-section duties into proof-backed resume bullets

## Caption

Your experience section should not read like a duty list.

Many student resumes say:

- Assisted marketing team
- Worked on data entry
- Helped with research
- Participated in project

These lines are not wrong.

They are just incomplete.

A recruiter is trying to answer one question:

What changed because you were there?

Rewrite one duty into four parts:

1. Context - what team or task was this?
2. Action - what did you actually do?
3. Tool - what did you use?
4. Result - what became faster, clearer, cleaner, or easier?

Before:
Worked on social media posts.

After:
Planned 12 Instagram posts for a campus fest using Canva and a content calendar, helping the team publish on schedule for 3 weeks.

You do not need a huge result.

Use honest proof.

Numbers help, but so do scope, frequency, users, team size, deadline, or output.

One clear line beats five vague duties.

Pick one experience bullet today.

Can a recruiter see your action and proof in 7 seconds?
