## Caption

Sunday is a good day to stop guessing which applications need work.

Before Monday applications, look at last week's shortlist through one simple lens:

Proof.

Not motivation.
Not more tabs.
Proof.

Pick the 5 roles you most wanted.

For each one, mark:
1. skill the role asked for
2. proof you showed
3. proof gap still visible

Most students discover the same problem.

The role asks for Excel, SQL, design, writing, or communication.

The application only says "interested" or "quick learner".

Fix one gap today.

Add one project line.
Rewrite one resume bullet.
Attach one relevant sample.
Update one LinkedIn section.

More applications help only when the proof is getting clearer.

If you review one role today, which proof gap would you fix first?

-- World of Interns
