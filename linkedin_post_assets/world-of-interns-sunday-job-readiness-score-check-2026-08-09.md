## Caption

Before Monday applications, do one Sunday job-readiness check.

Take one role you want.
Put its requirements on the left.
Put your visible proof on the right.

The useful question is not "Am I good enough?"
It is "Can a recruiter see the proof in 7 seconds?"

We often see the same gap:
students have done the project,
but the resume hides the result.

A better check:
- one matching skill
- one project or task that proves it
- one outcome, number, or concrete detail
- one line placed where the reader will see it first

If a requirement has no visible proof, do not rewrite the whole resume.
Add one clearer line before you apply.

What proof would you move higher on your resume this week?
