## Caption

Most fresher resume summaries are too wide to help.

"Motivated student seeking a challenging role..." tells a recruiter almost nothing.

The summary has one job:
help someone understand your fit in 5 seconds.

A better fresher summary answers 3 questions:

1. What role are you aiming for?
2. What proof do you already have?
3. What can you do on day one?

Try this structure:

Final-year B.Com student applying for finance internships.
Built two Excel dashboards for college events.
Comfortable with reconciliations, basic analysis, and clear reporting.

No adjectives.
No life story.
No promises you cannot prove.

If your summary could fit on any student's resume, rewrite it until it sounds like your work.

What is the first line of your resume saying right now?
