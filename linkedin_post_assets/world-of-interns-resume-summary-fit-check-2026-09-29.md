Topic: Tuesday Resume Review - resume summary fit check for freshers

## Caption

Your resume summary should not sound like a promise.

It should work like a map.

Most fresher summaries say:
"Hardworking student seeking an opportunity to learn and grow."

That line tells a recruiter almost nothing during the first scan.

A better summary answers three things fast:

- what role you are aiming for
- what proof already exists
- what tools or domain match the role

Weak:
"Motivated engineering student looking for a challenging internship."

Stronger:
"Final-year ECE student applying for data analyst internships. Built 2 Excel and Python dashboards, cleaned survey data for a college project, and comfortable with SQL basics."

No big claims.

Just useful context.

If you have no experience, do not fill the summary with personality words.

Use this 3-line test:
Role I want.
Proof I can show.
Tools I can discuss.

Then let your projects and skills carry the rest.

What does your current resume summary prove in the first 5 seconds?
