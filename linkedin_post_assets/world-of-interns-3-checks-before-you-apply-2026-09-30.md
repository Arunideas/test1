## Topic

3 checks before you apply to an internship listing

## Caption

Before you apply, read the internship post like a shortlist filter.

A good listing tells you three things clearly:

1. What work you will do.
2. Who will review it.
3. What proof you will have at the end.

If the post only says "marketing intern", "AI intern", or "business development intern", pause.

Ask for the project scope.
Check whether there is a mentor or reporting manager.
Look for stipend, duration, mode, tools, and selection steps.

This protects your time.
It also makes your application sharper because you can match your resume to the actual work.

One simple rule:
If you cannot explain the internship in one sentence after reading the post, verify before applying.
