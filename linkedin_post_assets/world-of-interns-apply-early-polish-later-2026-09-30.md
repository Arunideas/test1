Caption:

Most internship applications do not fail because they were sent one day late.

They fail because students wait for perfect.

A better rule:

Apply early when the role matches 70% of your profile.

Polish the note for the 3 details the company actually asked for.

Send, then improve the next application.

Do not use "early" as an excuse for copy-paste.

Do not use "polished" as an excuse to disappear for a week.

Recruiters shortlist while applications are still coming in.

A clear, relevant application today is often stronger than a perfect one after the first batch is closed.

Before you apply, check:
- role match
- proof you can show
- one tailored line
- deadline or rolling review

What do you usually wait to perfect before applying?
