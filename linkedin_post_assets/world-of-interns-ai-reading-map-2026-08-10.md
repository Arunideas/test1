## Caption

AI can help you read before an interview.

But it should not help you pretend.

If a company sends a 40-page report, do not ask AI for "everything I need to know."

Ask for a reading map instead.

1. What are the 5 sections I must read myself?
2. Which numbers should I verify in the original PDF?
3. What terms need definitions?
4. What questions can I ask after reading?

Then open the report.

Check the page numbers.

Mark the numbers.

Write your own 3-line summary.

Now AI has saved time without replacing your judgment.

This matters because interviews expose shallow preparation quickly.

A confident student is not the one who memorised a summary.

It is the one who knows which parts they checked.

What is one document you would use this reading-map method on?
