## Caption

Many fresher resumes have a skills section that looks strong at first.

Then the rest of the resume does not prove it.

That is where trust drops.

A simple resume check:

For every skill you list, add one proof line somewhere else on the resume.

Not a long paragraph.

Just a clear sign that you have used the skill.

Example:

Skill: Excel

Proof line: Cleaned 3 months of attendance data and built a weekly summary sheet.

Skill: Communication

Proof line: Wrote daily update notes for a 5-member project team.

Skill: React

Proof line: Built a responsive task board with filters, forms, and local storage.

The skill section tells the recruiter what to look for.

The proof line gives them a reason to believe it.

Before sending your next resume, pick the top 5 skills and ask:

Where is the evidence?

If there is no evidence, either add it or remove the skill.

That small edit can make a beginner resume feel much more honest and easier to trust.

Which skill on your resume needs a stronger proof line?
