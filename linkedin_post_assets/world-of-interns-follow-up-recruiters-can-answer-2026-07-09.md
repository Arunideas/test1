## Caption

Most follow-up messages fail because they ask the recruiter to do the work again.

"Any update?" gives no context.

A better follow-up makes the decision easy to reopen.

Try this structure:

Role: the exact internship title
Context: when you applied or interviewed
Proof: one line that matches the role
Ask: one clear next step

Example:

Hi [Name], I applied for the Marketing Intern role on Monday. I saw the role needs short-form content and basic analytics. I have been running my college club page and tracking weekly reach in a simple sheet. If the role is still open, is there anything else I should send?

This is not pushy.

It reminds the recruiter where you fit without pretending urgency.

Small detail, but it matters:
do not follow up every day.

Give the process room.
Make your message easy to answer.
Then move to the next application.

What is one follow-up line you have used that felt natural?
