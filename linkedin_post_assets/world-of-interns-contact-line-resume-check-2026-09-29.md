## Caption

A resume can be strong and still lose a callback because the contact line is messy.

Recruiters do not want to hunt.

Before they read your projects, they check one simple thing:
can they contact you quickly and verify your work?

Keep the top line boring.

Use:
- name
- city
- phone
- professional email
- LinkedIn profile
- GitHub or portfolio, if it proves work

Remove:
- full address
- school email that may expire
- broken links
- usernames that look casual
- icons that disappear in parsers

Then do a 30-second test.

Open every link from the PDF.
Copy the header into plain text.
Send it to yourself once.

If the first line creates friction, the rest of the resume has to work harder.

What would you check first?

-- World of Interns
