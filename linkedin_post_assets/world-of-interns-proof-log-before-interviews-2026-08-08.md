Caption:

Most students forget their best proof right before an interview.

Not because they did nothing.
Because they never saved the small details while the work was fresh.

A simple proof log helps.

After every project, task, event, or internship week, write three lines:

1. What problem did I touch?
2. What did I actually do?
3. What changed after that?

Do this for four weeks and you stop saying:
"I worked on a project."

You start saying:
"I cleaned a messy spreadsheet, grouped 180 records, and helped the team find duplicate entries faster."

That sounds small.
It also sounds real.

Recruiters do not need a perfect story.
They need enough proof to trust the story.

If you are applying this month, open one note today.
Add three proof lines from your last project.

Your next interview answer becomes easier to remember.

What is one small proof point from your week that you could save today?
