💼 Internship Opportunities

Most students do not lose internships because they apply late.

They lose time on the wrong openings.

Before applying today, do one 10-minute check:

1. Is the role asking for work you can prove with one project?
2. Is the company page, website, and email domain consistent?
3. Is the stipend or workload realistic for an internship?
4. Can you explain why this role fits your next skill goal?

A strong application list is not 50 random links.

It is 8 to 12 roles where your resume, portfolio, and learning path actually match the work.

Takeaway: Apply slower for 10 minutes so you can apply better for the next 2 hours.

Which filter should students use first?

[ ] Skill match

[ ] Stipend realism
