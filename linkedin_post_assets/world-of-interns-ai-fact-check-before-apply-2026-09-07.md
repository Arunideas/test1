## Caption

AI can help you check an application.

It cannot be your source of truth.

Many students paste a job description into an AI tool and send the first rewrite.

That is where mistakes enter:

- skills you do not actually have
- achievements that sound bigger than they are
- generic lines that could fit any company
- missing proof for the role's main requirement

Use AI as a fact-checker before you apply.

Try this:

1. Paste the job description.
2. Paste your resume or project notes.
3. Ask for three columns: claim, proof, needs verification.
4. Remove every claim that has no real proof.
5. Rewrite only the lines you can defend in an interview.

The useful question is not:

"Can AI make this sound better?"

It is:

"Which line would I struggle to prove if a recruiter asked me about it?"

Better applications are not louder.

They are easier to trust.

What do you check before you apply?

-- World of Interns
