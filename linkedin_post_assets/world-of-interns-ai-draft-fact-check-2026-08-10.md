## Caption

AI can clean a sentence.

It cannot create proof you do not have.

Before you use an AI draft for a resume, SOP, cold email, or assignment, run one small check:

Ask: "Can I prove every sentence if someone questions it?"

Then mark each claim as:

1. Evidence
2. Opinion
3. Invented

Keep the evidence.
Rewrite the opinion.
Delete the invented part.

Bad line:
"Improved user engagement through strategic analysis."

Safer line:
"Reviewed 42 survey responses and grouped the top 3 onboarding issues."

The second line is not louder.
It is easier to defend.

Use AI for clarity.
Keep responsibility for accuracy.

What would you ask AI to fact-check before sending an application?

-- World of Interns
