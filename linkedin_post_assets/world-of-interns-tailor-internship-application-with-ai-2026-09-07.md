## Caption

A generic internship application is easy to send.

It is also easy to ignore.

Before you apply, use AI for one small job: make your proof match the role.

Not to fake experience.

To find the real evidence you already have.

Try this 8-minute check:

1. Paste the internship description.
2. Paste your rough resume or project notes.
3. Ask: "Which 3 parts of my work directly support this role? Quote the evidence. Do not invent anything."
4. Rewrite only the bullets that have proof.
5. Delete anything you cannot explain in an interview.

Your final application should answer one question:

"Why does this student make sense for this role?"

If the answer is not visible in the first screen, the recruiter has to work too hard.

Use AI to make relevance clearer.

Not to make your profile look bigger.

What part of your application still sounds too generic?
