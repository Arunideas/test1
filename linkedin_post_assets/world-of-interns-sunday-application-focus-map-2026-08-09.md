Topic: Sunday application focus map before Monday applications

## Caption

Before Monday applications, do a Sunday focus map.

Not a 20-tab tracker.

Just one page that tells you where your next hour should go.

Column 1: roles worth applying to.

Write the three internships that still look relevant after reading the work, stipend, location, and eligibility carefully.

Column 2: proof that is already ready.

For each role, mark the strongest visible proof you can show:

A project.
A result.
A writing sample.
A GitHub link.
A presentation.
A small analysis.

Column 3: proof that needs repair.

This is the important part.

Maybe your resume says "worked on data analysis" but does not say what you analysed.

Maybe your project has a GitHub link but no README.

Maybe your profile says communication skills but has no example of writing.

Fix one proof gap before sending five more applications.

A useful Sunday review should reduce Monday noise.

Your target is not "apply everywhere."

It is:

3 relevant roles.
3 proof lines checked.
1 weak signal improved.

That is enough to make Monday calmer and sharper.

What is the one proof gap you can fix before your next application?
