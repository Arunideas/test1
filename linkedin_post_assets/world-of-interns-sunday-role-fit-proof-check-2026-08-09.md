## Caption

A useful Sunday review is not:
“How many internships did I apply to?”

A better question is:
“Can a recruiter see proof that I fit the role?”

Many student applications lose strength in the same place.
The resume lists skills.
The role asks for proof.
But the matching project, result, or tool usage is hidden three lines later.

Before Monday applications, try a simple two-column check:

Column 1: What the role asks for
Column 2: Where my resume proves it

If a role asks for Excel, show the report you cleaned.
If it asks for communication, show the client note, documentation, or presentation.
If it asks for Python, show the project outcome, not just the word Python.

Then write one short role-fit note before applying:

“I noticed this role needs ___. My project/work in ___ is the closest proof I have.”

It is not about applying to more roles.
It is about making the right proof easier to find.

What would you add to a Sunday application review?
