## Topic

Verify before you apply: a 3-minute internship check

## Caption

Before you apply, do one small check.

A real internship should make the work visible.

Look for:

1. A company page with recent activity.
2. A role description with actual tasks.
3. A mentor or reporting line.
4. Stipend, duration, and weekly hours.
5. No fee, deposit, or certificate-first promise.

If two of these are missing, pause.

Ask one polite question:

"Could you share who will review the work and what the first two weeks look like?"

Genuine teams answer clearly.
Fake listings usually become vague or pushy.

Your time is part of the cost.
Protect it before you send your resume.

What would you check before applying?
