# World of Interns LinkedIn post - 2026-08-09

## Topic
Sunday Weekly Report: a practical skill-gap reset before Monday applications.

## Caption
Most students do not need a longer resume on Sunday.

They need a cleaner gap map.

Before sending Monday applications, check 3 things:

1. Role fit
Does the role ask for skills you can prove with one project, assignment, internship, or certificate?

2. Proof depth
Can a recruiter see what you built, what you changed, and what result came from it?

3. Next edit
Is there one line on your resume that can be made more specific before you apply?

This is a small weekly report, not a full career plan.

Pick 5 roles.
Mark the missing proof.
Fix one line before applying.

It keeps the week practical.

What would you add?
