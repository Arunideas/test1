## Caption

If a resume bullet needs 20 seconds to understand, most recruiters will not give it 20 seconds.

A weak bullet says:

"Worked on a machine learning project."

It tells the reader the topic.

Not the work.

Not the result.

Not why it matters.

A stronger fresher bullet has three parts:

1. What you built or improved
2. What tools or skills you used
3. What changed because of it

For example:

"Built a student attendance dashboard in React and Firebase, reducing manual tracking for a 60-student class."

Even if the project is small, the bullet now shows useful work.

Before sending your next resume, pick one project bullet.

Ask:

Can a recruiter see the action, tool, and result in one line?

If not, rewrite that bullet before editing the design.

Which resume bullet would you improve first?
