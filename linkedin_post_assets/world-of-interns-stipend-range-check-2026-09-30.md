# World of Interns LinkedIn Post - 2026-09-30

## Topic

Read the stipend range before you apply to an internship

## Caption

An internship stipend range is not just a number.

It tells you what to verify before you give time to the process.

If a post says:
"Rs 5,000 to Rs 20,000 per month"
do not read only the top number.

Check five things:

1. Fixed amount vs performance-linked amount.
2. Monthly payout date.
3. Reimbursements included or separate.
4. Duration and expected hours.
5. Whether unpaid training comes first.

A clear listing will answer most of this before the final round.

A vague listing will make you ask after you have already spent time.

Before applying, send one calm question:
"Can you confirm the fixed stipend, payout cycle, duration, and work mode?"

Good companies do not mind clarity.
Students should not have to guess the basics.

What is the first stipend detail you check?
