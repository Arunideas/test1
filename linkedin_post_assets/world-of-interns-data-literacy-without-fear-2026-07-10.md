Friday Future Skill: data literacy for students who are scared of maths.

## Caption

Data literacy is not about loving maths.

It is about not getting fooled by numbers.

In internships, numbers show up everywhere:

A campaign report.
A survey result.
A spreadsheet.
A product dashboard.
A resume metric someone copied without understanding.

If you can ask three simple questions, you are already ahead:

1. What exactly is this number measuring?
2. Compared with what?
3. What decision would change because of it?

A percentage without a base can mislead.
An average can hide extremes.
A chart can look impressive and still answer the wrong question.

You do not need advanced statistics to start.

You need the habit of slowing down before you trust a number.

Next time you see a metric in a case study, project, or internship report, write one line:

"This number matters because..."

If you cannot finish that sentence, you do not understand the number yet.

Which number do students misunderstand most in resumes or projects?
