## Topic

Friday Future Skill: Data literacy for people who are scared of maths.

## Caption

Data literacy is not the same as being "good at maths".

For most interns, it starts much smaller.

Can you read a table without guessing?
Can you spot when a percentage is hiding the real story?
Can you ask what was counted, what was skipped, and who the data represents?

That skill is becoming useful in almost every role.

Marketing interns look at campaign numbers.
HR interns look at hiring funnels.
Operations interns look at delays.
Product interns look at user behaviour.

You do not need to become a statistician first.

Start with one simple practice this week:

1. Pick any public chart or spreadsheet.
2. Write the main claim in one sentence.
3. Write one question the data does not answer.
4. Write one decision you would not make from it yet.

This trains judgement.

And judgement is what separates "I saw a number" from "I understood what it means".

What would you add?
