Post copy:

Data literacy does not start with formulas.

It starts with asking:

"What is this number trying to say?"

Many students avoid data-heavy work because they think data means advanced maths.

But in most internships, the first useful skill is simpler.

Can you spot a missing value?
Can you compare two numbers honestly?
Can you ask why a metric changed before making a claim?
Can you explain a chart without hiding behind jargon?

That habit matters in marketing, HR, finance, operations, product, and AI work.

A small practice for this week:

1. Pick one public report or dashboard.
2. Write down the main number.
3. Ask what was counted, what was ignored, and who collected it.
4. Explain the number in five plain sentences.
5. Add one caution before you share it.

This is not about becoming a data scientist overnight.

It is about becoming the intern who does not panic when work comes with numbers.

What is one number you see often but have never really questioned?
