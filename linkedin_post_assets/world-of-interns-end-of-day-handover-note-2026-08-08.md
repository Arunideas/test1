## Topic

Saturday Student Story: a simple end-of-day handover note interns can send when work is still unfinished, so managers see progress, blockers, and the next clear step.

## Caption

Not every internship task gets finished before the day ends.

That is normal.

What matters is whether your manager can see what happened.

A useful habit:

Send a 4-line handover note before you log off.

1. What changed today
2. What is still open
3. Where you got stuck
4. What you will try next

This is not about proving you worked hard.

It helps your manager decide whether to wait, unblock, redirect, or pair you with someone.

It also protects you from a common intern mistake:

disappearing with the task until it is perfect.

Most teams do not need perfect silence.
They need visible progress.

If you are in your first internship, try the note for one week.
Keep it short enough that someone can read it in 20 seconds.
