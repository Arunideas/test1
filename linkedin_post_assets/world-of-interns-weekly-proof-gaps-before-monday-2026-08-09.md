## Caption

This week's application review had one quiet lesson:

clear proof beats more claims.

The strongest student profiles did not try to look complete.

They made three parts easy to verify.

1. Resume bullets connected work to an outcome.
2. Project links opened to a short explanation, not only a repo.
3. Interview notes used examples from real practice, not memorised answers.

This is useful before Monday because small proof gaps are fixable.

Pick one application and check:

"Can a recruiter see what I did, why it mattered, and where the proof is?"

If not, add one line, one link, or one example.

A cleaner proof trail can make the same experience feel much easier to trust.

What is one proof gap you can close today?
