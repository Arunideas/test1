## Caption

Your first internship mistake does not usually define you.

The next 20 minutes often do.

Maybe you sent the wrong file.

Maybe you missed one instruction.

Maybe you said "done" before checking the last detail.

That moment feels bigger than it is.

But a team does not only notice the mistake.

They notice your recovery pattern.

Try this simple loop:

1. Pause: check what actually happened.
2. Own: say what went wrong without a long excuse.
3. Fix: share the next action and by when.
4. Follow up: confirm once it is corrected.

A useful message can be short:

"I noticed the file I sent had the old version. I am replacing it with the correct one now and will re-check the link before sending the update."

That is calm.

That is responsible.

That helps people trust you again faster.

Internships are not tests where one small mistake ends the story.

They are places where people watch how you learn in real time.

What is one recovery habit every intern should practice?
