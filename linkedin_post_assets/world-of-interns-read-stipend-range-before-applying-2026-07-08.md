## Caption

Before you apply, read the stipend line slowly.

A stipend range is not just a number.
It tells you how clear the internship really is.

Look for 3 things.

1. Is it fixed or "up to"?
If the post says "up to 10,000", ask what decides the final amount.

2. Are expenses separate?
Travel, internet, or field work costs can quietly reduce the real stipend.

3. When is it paid?
Monthly, after completion, or only after targets are met are very different.

A good listing does not make students guess.
It explains the work, duration, stipend, and payment terms before asking for effort.

If the stipend line feels vague, save the post and ask one clear question before applying.
