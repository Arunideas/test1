# World of Interns LinkedIn post - 2026-08-10

## Topic

Monday AI Tool: using AI to rewrite a cold email without sounding robotic.

## Caption

AI can help with cold emails.

But it often adds sentences students would never say.

The problem is not using AI.
The problem is sending the first polished draft.

Before you send, run a human check:

1. Would I say this aloud?
2. Is the ask clear in one line?
3. Did I mention one real reason for reaching out?
4. Did AI add praise I cannot defend?

A good cold email for internships does not need big words.

It needs context, proof, and a small next step.

Try this prompt:

"Rewrite this email in a simple student voice. Keep it under 120 words. Remove exaggerated praise. Keep one clear ask."

Then read it once without the prompt.

If it sounds like a brochure, edit it again.

What is one phrase you always delete from AI-written emails?
