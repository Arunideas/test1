Caption:

Your education section is not just for college name and CGPA.

For a fresher resume, it can answer one useful question:
did you use college time to build job-relevant proof?

Keep the basics short:
degree, college, year, CGPA if it helps.

Then add only 2-3 proof points that match the role:
- relevant coursework
- one lab/project with tools
- competition, paper, or leadership only if it shows useful work

Avoid filling half the page with school marks, seminars, and every certificate.

A recruiter scanning in 7 seconds should understand:
what you studied,
what you practiced,
and why it connects to the internship.

Small test: remove any education line that does not help the recruiter shortlist you.

What would you keep in your education section?
