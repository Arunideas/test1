## Topic

Remote vs in-office internships for a first role

## Caption

Remote internship or in-office internship?

For a first role, the better option is usually the one where feedback is easier to get.

Remote can work if the company has clear tasks, a named mentor, weekly review calls, and written expectations.

In-office can work if you are not just sitting near people, but actually getting tasks, reviews, and context.

Before saying yes, ask three simple questions:

1. Who reviews my work every week?
2. What will I ship or complete in the first 30 days?
3. How will performance be measured?

If the answer is vague, location will not save the internship.

A strong first internship should leave you with proof of work, not only attendance.

If you are choosing between remote and in-office, what would you ask before accepting?
