## Caption

Applying to more internships is not always the best Sunday plan.

Sometimes the better plan is to reduce the list.

Before Monday, open the roles you saved this week and make a small shortlist.

Use three checks:

1. Can you explain why this role fits your current skills?
2. Can your resume show one piece of matching proof?
3. Can you apply without rushing the same weak version everywhere?

If the answer is no, keep the role in a later list.

If the answer is yes, give that application proper attention.

A focused shortlist helps you spend less time guessing and more time improving the few applications that actually make sense.

This is also a useful weekly report for yourself:

What did I save?
What is worth applying to?
What proof do I need to add before I send it?

Small review. Better applications.

Before the new week starts, which saved role deserves your best version?
