## Post copy

When 500 applications land for one internship, HR does not read like a professor.

They scan for reasons to continue.

First screen usually means:
1. role-fit keywords
2. one relevant project or experience
3. proof that the work was finished
4. a resume that is easy to skim

The quiet rejection happens when a good student makes the recruiter assemble the story.

Try this before you apply:
read the JD once, then put the clearest matching proof in the top half of your resume.

Not more claims.
More visible evidence.

If a recruiter had 7 seconds with your resume, what would they understand first?
