Sunday applications feel productive.

But a full tracker can still hide the real problem.

Many students apply to more roles before checking whether their proof matches the role.

So before Monday, run a 10-minute job-readiness scorecard.

Pick five internships you actually want.

For each one, write three lines:

1. The role skill they are asking for
2. The proof you already have
3. The proof gap you can fix this week

If the gap is "no proof", do not panic.

Fix one small thing.

Add a GitHub README.
Rewrite one resume bullet with a result.
Record a 60-second project walkthrough.
Complete one role-relevant practice task.

The goal is not to become perfect by Monday.

The goal is to send fewer applications with clearer evidence.

That is usually a better weekly report than "applied to 40 roles".

What is one proof gap you can close before your next application?
