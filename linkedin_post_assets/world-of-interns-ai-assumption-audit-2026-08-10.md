## Caption

Before you start an internship task, ask AI one quiet question:

"What assumptions am I making?"

Many students do the work fast.

Then they discover the brief meant something slightly different.

The problem is not effort.

The problem is starting with hidden assumptions.

Try this before your next task:

Paste the task brief into AI.

Add what you understood in your own words.

Ask it to separate:

1. what is clearly stated
2. what is implied
3. what is missing
4. what could be interpreted two ways
5. what you should confirm before starting

Then write your own short clarification message to your manager or mentor.

Do not use AI to avoid thinking.

Use it to notice where your thinking is still loose.

That one step can save hours of rework.

It also shows a habit employers value:

You do not just complete tasks.

You confirm the problem before solving it.

What is one assumption you wish you had checked earlier in an internship or project?
