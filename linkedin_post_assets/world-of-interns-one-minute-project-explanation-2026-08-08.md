Topic: One-minute project explanation for interviews

## Caption

Most students explain projects by listing features.

Recruiters usually need a shorter signal:
what problem did you solve,
what decision did you make,
what changed because of it?

Try a one-minute project answer.

1. Problem: "Our college club was tracking attendance manually."
2. Choice: "I built a simple form and dashboard because the team needed weekly visibility."
3. Result: "It reduced duplicate entries and made follow-up easier."

This is not about sounding advanced.
It is about making your work easy to understand quickly.

Before your next interview, pick one project and write these three lines.

If a non-technical friend can repeat it back, your explanation is probably clear.

What part of your project is hardest to explain?
