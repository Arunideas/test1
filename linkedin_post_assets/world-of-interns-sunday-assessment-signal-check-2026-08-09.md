Topic: Sunday assessment signal check before Monday applications

## Caption

A useful Sunday report does not need a big dashboard.

For students applying to internships, it can start with three small signals from the roles and assessments you saw this week.

Signal 1: which skills kept appearing?

Not the full job description.
Just the repeated verbs.

Analyse.
Write.
Debug.
Present.
Research.

Signal 2: where is your proof visible?

If the role asks for analysis, does your resume show one project where you analysed something?

If the role asks for communication, does your profile show one example of writing, explaining, or presenting?

Signal 3: what needs one hour of practice before Monday?

One SQL question.
One Excel task.
One short product teardown.
One cleaner project explanation.

This is where many students lose momentum.

They keep applying with the same material, even when the market is quietly telling them what to improve.

Before Monday applications, try this:

Pick one role you actually want.
Circle the top three skills it asks for.
Write one proof line for each skill.
Move the strongest proof higher on your resume.
Practise the weakest skill for one focused hour.

That is not a full career plan.

It is just a better Monday starting point.

Which skill kept showing up in your internship search this week?
