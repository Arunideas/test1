# World of Interns LinkedIn post - 2026-08-08

## Topic

Saturday Student Story: keep a short decision trail on your first internship assignment so managers can review your thinking, not just the final output.

## Caption

An intern does not need to sound certain on day one.

They do need to make their thinking easy to review.

A small habit I like:

Before sending the first version of a task, add a decision trail:

1. What I understood the task to be
2. The option I chose
3. Why I chose it
4. Where I am least sure

That note changes the conversation.

A manager can correct the direction early.
A teammate can spot a missing constraint.
The intern learns how decisions are made inside the team.

The output matters.
The thinking behind it is often what helps people coach you faster.

If you are starting an internship, keep the trail short.
Four lines is enough.

What is one small habit that helped you learn faster in a new role?
