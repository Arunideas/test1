Prompt skills alone are not a career.

They are useful, but they are not enough.

A student who can ask AI for an answer still has to know what problem is worth solving.

They need to spot when output is shallow.

They need to test whether the answer works in a real task.

That is the career skill: judgment.

A simple check before calling yourself AI-ready:

1. Can you define the task without AI?
2. Can you explain why the answer is right?
3. Can you turn the output into something usable?

If the answer is no, practice the work behind the prompt.

Use AI.

But build proof that you can think, test, and ship.
