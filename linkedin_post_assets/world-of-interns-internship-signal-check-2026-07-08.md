## Caption

Before you apply to an internship, spend 6 minutes checking the signal.

Not every listing deserves your resume.

A useful listing normally answers five basic questions:

1. What will I work on?
2. Who will review my work?
3. Is there a stipend, duration, and location?
4. What skill does this internship help me practice?
5. What proof will I have at the end: project, report, reference, or offer path?

If a post only says "great learning opportunity" and hides the basics, pause.

Students do not need more applications. They need better filters.

Save this checklist before your next application.

What would you add?

-- World of Interns
