## Caption

Before Monday applications, make a proof ladder.

Not a full resume rewrite.
Just a 10-minute check.

Take one role.
Write the top 3 things it asks for.

Then place one proof line under each:
- where you used the skill
- what you built, handled, or improved
- what changed because of your work

Now rank those proof lines.

The strongest proof should not sit at the bottom of the resume.
It should be visible before the reader starts scanning quickly.

Many students lose attention here.
The experience exists.
The proof is just buried.

This Sunday, move one strong proof line higher before applying on Monday.

Which proof line on your resume deserves a better position?
