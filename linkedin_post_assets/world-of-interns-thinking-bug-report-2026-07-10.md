## Caption

Most students try to memorise answers before an interview.

That helps until the question changes.

A better skill is debugging your own thinking.

When you get an answer wrong, do not only write the correct answer.

Ask:
1. What assumption did I make?
2. Which clue did I ignore?
3. How would I test this next time?

This works for aptitude, coding, case questions, and even AI tools.

Because real work rarely asks the exact question you practised.

It asks you to notice where your logic is weak and fix it calmly.

One small exercise:
After every mock test or project bug, write a 3-line thinking bug report:
- I assumed...
- I missed...
- Next time I will check...

Memorising gives speed.

Debugging your thinking gives range.

What has helped you learn from mistakes faster?
