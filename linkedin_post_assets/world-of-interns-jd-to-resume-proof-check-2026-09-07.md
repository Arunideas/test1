## Caption

Before you apply, ask AI to do one boring but useful thing.

Turn the job description into a resume proof checklist.

Most students paste the JD into AI and ask for a cover letter.

That is usually too late.

First, paste the JD and your current resume.

Then ask:

1. Which 5 skills does this role repeat or imply?
2. Where does my resume already prove each skill?
3. Which claims are weak because they have no project, result, or tool attached?
4. What one line should I rewrite before applying?

The point is not to let AI invent experience.

The point is to catch the gap between what the company asked for and what your resume actually proves.

A small example:

"Worked on data analysis project"

can become:

"Cleaned 4,000 survey rows in Python and built a dashboard to compare weekly response trends."

Same student.

Clearer proof.

Before sending your next application, run this check once.

What would you add to this checklist?
