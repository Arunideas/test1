Topic: Monday AI Tool - using AI as a mock-interview feedback loop before applying or interviewing.

## Caption

AI can mock-interview you.

But the useful part is not the score it gives at the end.

The useful part is the second round.

Try this:

1. Paste the internship description.
2. Ask for 8 role-specific questions.
3. Answer out loud, without notes.
4. Ask AI to mark only missing proof, vague claims, and unclear examples.

Then rewrite one answer.

Do not let the tool invent experience.
Do not accept compliments you cannot verify.

A good mock interview should leave you with sharper examples, not just confidence.

What interview answer are you practising this week?
