## Topic

When a free internship is worth it, and when it is not

## Caption

An unpaid internship is not automatically bad.

But a vague unpaid internship is a warning sign.

Before you say yes, check what you are really getting in return.

Ask five simple questions:

1. What work will I actually do each week?
2. Who will review my work?
3. How many hours are expected?
4. What skill or portfolio proof will I leave with?
5. Can I exit if the role becomes only admin work?

"Exposure" is not enough.

A good unpaid role should still have structure, feedback, and a clear learning outcome.

A weak one will avoid details and make the student feel grateful for any task.

Your time has value even when money is not involved.

Before accepting, ask:
"What will a successful first month look like?"

The answer will tell you more than the title.
