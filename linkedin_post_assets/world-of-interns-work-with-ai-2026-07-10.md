## Caption

AI will not replace the student who can think with it.

It will expose the student who only copies from it.

A useful future skill is not prompting faster.
It is knowing when the answer is weak.

Before you use any AI output in an assignment, resume bullet, or project plan, run three checks:

1. What is the task really asking?
2. Which part of the answer is assumed, not proven?
3. What would change if the opposite were true?

This takes 5 minutes.
It saves you from vague work, copied language, and project explanations that fall apart in an interview.

Use AI like a teammate.
Question it.
Edit it.
Make the final judgment yourself.

Which skill are you learning right now?
