## Caption

Before you send more internship applications on Monday, pause for 15 minutes.

Do a relevance review.

Most students do not lose opportunities only because their resume is weak.

They lose attention because the recruiter cannot quickly connect three things:

1. What role they want
2. What proof they already have
3. What gap they are fixing next

This is a useful Sunday check.

Pick 3 internships you want to apply for tomorrow.

For each one, write one line:

"This role needs ___, and my proof is ___."

If the second blank feels unclear, do not rush the application.

Fix one small proof gap first.

That could be a project note, a GitHub README, a portfolio line, a short case study, or a clearer resume bullet.

One relevant application with visible proof is often stronger than ten applications sent without a signal.

What proof gap will you close before your next internship application?
