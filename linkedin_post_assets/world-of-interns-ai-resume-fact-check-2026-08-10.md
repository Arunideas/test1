Topic: Use AI to fact-check resume bullets before sending an internship application.

## Caption

AI can make a resume bullet sound polished.

It can also make it false.

Before you paste an AI-written line into your resume, run one more prompt:

"Act like a careful interviewer.
Fact-check these resume bullets.
For each one, tell me:
1. what proof I must have
2. what result I should be able to explain
3. which skill I should be ready to demonstrate
4. which claim sounds stronger than my evidence
Do not make the bullets sound better. Make them safer."

Then look at the answer slowly.

If AI says your bullet needs a metric, but you do not have one, do not invent it.

If it says "built an end-to-end system", ask yourself whether you can explain the data flow, edge cases, and what broke.

If it finds a claim you cannot defend in an interview, remove that claim.

A good resume line is not the most impressive version.

It is the clearest true version.

Try this on one project today:

"Built a dashboard using React and APIs."

Ask AI what proof would make it stronger, and what wording you should avoid if you cannot show usage or outcomes.

Then write the final bullet in your own words.

Would every line on your resume survive a live interview?
