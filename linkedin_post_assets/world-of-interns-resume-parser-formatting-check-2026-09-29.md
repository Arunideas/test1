## Caption

Your resume can look clean and still be hard to scan.

One quiet problem is formatting.

Tables, text boxes, icons, two-column layouts, and skill bars often look neat on screen.
But many recruiters first see a parsed version inside an ATS or inbox preview.

If the parser reads your project title after the tools list, or drops the date, your best proof becomes harder to trust.

Do a 2-minute check:

1. Copy the resume text from the PDF.
2. Paste it into a plain text note.
3. Read it from top to bottom.
4. Fix anything that appears in the wrong order.

A simple one-column resume is not boring.
It is easier to verify in 7 seconds.

Would you try this before your next application?

-- World of Interns
