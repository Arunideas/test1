## Topic

What a good internship description should actually tell you

## Caption

Before you apply, read the internship description like a short contract.

A good listing should answer 4 things:

1. What work will you actually do?
Not "assist the team".
A real task, project, or outcome.

2. Which skills will you use or learn?
If every skill in the world is listed, the role is probably unclear.

3. Who will guide or review your work?
For a first internship, feedback matters as much as the task.

4. What are the basic terms?
Duration.
Stipend.
Location.
Work hours.
Selection process.

If a post hides all of this, do not rush.
Ask one clear question before sending your resume.

A serious internship does not need fancy words.
It needs clear expectations.
