## Caption

The students who get trusted fastest are not always the loudest in the room.

A first internship tests one quiet skill: reliability.

Not "I know everything."

More:

"I said I would send the draft by 5 pm, and I did."

"I got stuck, so I asked with context."

"I made a mistake, then left a note so it is not repeated."

Teams remember this.

Brilliance helps when the problem is hard.

Reliability helps every day before the hard problem appears.

If you are starting your first internship, try a simple 4-line end-of-day update:

1. What I finished
2. What is blocked
3. What I will do next
4. Where I need input

It looks small.

It tells your manager: this person is safe to trust with real work.

What habit helped you build trust at work?
