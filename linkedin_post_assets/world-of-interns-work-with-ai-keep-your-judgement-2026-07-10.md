# World of Interns LinkedIn Post - Work with AI, Keep Your Judgement

AI will not replace the student who can think clearly with it.

It will expose the student who only copies from it.

A useful future skill is not prompt tricks.
It is learning how to work beside a tool without giving away your judgement.

Before you use AI for an assignment, resume bullet, project idea, or email, pause for four checks:

1. What do I already know?
2. What am I asking the tool to improve?
3. Which part must I verify myself?
4. Can I explain the final answer in my own words?

That last question matters.

If you cannot explain it, you have not learned it.
You have only outsourced the difficult part.

For interns, the strongest habit is simple:
use AI to make your thinking visible, not to hide the absence of it.

Where do you think students need the most help - using AI better, or checking AI better?
