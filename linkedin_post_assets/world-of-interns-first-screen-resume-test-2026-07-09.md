Topic: The first screen resume test recruiters use before reading details.

## Caption

Most recruiters do not read your resume from top to bottom first.

They scan the first screen.

Name.
Role direction.
Education or strongest project.
One proof line.
Skills that match the role.

If that area is crowded with a long summary, a generic objective, and every tool you have touched, the real signal gets buried.

A simple fix:

Put the role you want near the top.
Move your strongest relevant project above weaker details.
Add one result or proof line.
Keep skills grouped, not scattered.

This does not make an average resume perfect.

It makes the first decision easier: "Is this student worth a closer look?"

Before your next application, cover the lower half of your resume for 10 seconds.

Does the top half still explain your fit?
