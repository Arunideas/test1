Students who get callbacks usually do one boring thing differently.

They stop treating every opening like the same opening.

Before applying, they spend 10 minutes checking:
- what the role actually asks for
- which 2 projects prove that skill
- one line in their resume that should move up
- one sentence that explains why this company fits

This does not make the application fancy.
It makes it easier to understand.

Mass-applying feels productive because the number goes up.
But recruiters do not read your effort count.
They read the match they can see in 7 seconds.

A smaller list with clearer fit often beats a larger list with the same generic resume.

If you are applying this weekend, try this:
Pick 5 roles.
Write the exact skill each one repeats.
Move your closest proof near the top before you apply.

Which part of applying takes you the longest right now?
