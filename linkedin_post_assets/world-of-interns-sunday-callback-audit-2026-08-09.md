## LinkedIn caption

Before a new application week, review last week's silent applications.

Not to blame yourself.
To spot one pattern you can fix.

Open five roles you applied to.

Next to each one, write three short notes:

1. What the role asked for.
2. What your resume showed first.
3. What was missing, unclear, or hidden too low.

If the same gap appears again and again, that is this week's edit.

Maybe your project result is buried.
Maybe the skill is named differently.
Maybe every note says "interested" but never says why this company.

A quiet Sunday audit can make Monday applications less random.

What is one pattern you noticed in your applications this week?
