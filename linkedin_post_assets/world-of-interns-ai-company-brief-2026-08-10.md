## Caption

Before you apply, ask AI for a company research brief.

Not a paragraph to paste into your cover letter.

A 10-minute check to decide whether the internship deserves your time.

Use this prompt:

"Act like a calm career researcher. From this company website or job post, give me:
1. what the team seems to build
2. what problems this intern may touch
3. 3 skills I should prove in my resume
4. 2 honest questions to ask before applying
Flag anything you are unsure about."

Then do the important part.

Open the company website yourself.

Check one product page, one LinkedIn post, and the job description.

If AI cannot point back to a real line, do not trust that point.

Now write a two-line application note in your own words:

"I noticed your team works on ____.
My project on ____ is relevant because ____."

This is a better use of AI than asking it to write a generic cover letter.

It helps you apply to fewer roles with more signal.

What is one company you would research this way before applying?
