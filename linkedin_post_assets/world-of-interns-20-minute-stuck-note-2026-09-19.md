## Caption

Getting stuck in an internship is normal.

Staying silent until the deadline is the risk.

A useful rule:

If you are stuck for 20 minutes, write a small note before asking for help.

1. What I was trying to do
2. What I already checked
3. What happened instead
4. Two options I am considering
5. The exact question I need answered

This does two things.

It shows effort.

It makes it easier for your mentor to help.

"Please help" creates extra work for the other person.

"I tried A and B, this error remains, should I choose X or Y?" creates a clear next step.

Most good interns are not the ones who never get stuck.

They are the ones who make their work visible early.
