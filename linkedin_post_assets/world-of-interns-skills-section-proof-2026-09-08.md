## Caption

Your skills section should not look like a keyword dump.

Recruiters do not read every tool name equally.
They look for proof that one or two skills were used in real work.

A weak version:
Python, SQL, Excel, Tableau, Power BI, Machine Learning, Communication, Leadership.

A stronger version:
Python + SQL - cleaned 12,000 rows and built a placement dashboard for 3 departments.
Excel - tracked weekly sales data and found 2 reporting errors.
Communication - presented project findings to a 4-member review panel.

Keep the skills list short.
Then attach proof in projects, internships, or coursework.

Before you apply today, pick one skill from your resume and ask:
"Where is the evidence?"

What would you remove from an overcrowded skills section?
