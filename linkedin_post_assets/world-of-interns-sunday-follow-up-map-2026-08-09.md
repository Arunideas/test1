```text
Before the new internship week starts, do one quiet reset:

Make a follow-up map.

Not a huge networking tracker.
Not a complicated spreadsheet.
Just three short columns:

1. Conversations
Who did I speak to, email, message, interview with, or learn from this week?

2. Lessons
What did each interaction teach me about a role, skill, company, or gap in my preparation?

3. Next nudge
What is the smallest useful follow-up?

That follow-up might be:

- sending a thank-you note
- sharing a portfolio link
- asking one specific question
- updating a resume bullet
- practicing the skill that came up twice

Many students lose momentum because every interaction stays isolated.

A recruiter reply, a mentor comment, an interview question, or a classmate's advice can all become evidence for the next move.

Sunday night is a good time to connect those dots.

Choose one conversation from this week.
Write the lesson.
Send or schedule the next useful nudge.

Small follow-ups compound faster than forgotten conversations.

#WorldOfInterns #Internships #CareerAdvice #StudentCareers #Networking
```
