## Caption

Before you paste an AI-polished answer into an internship application, run one more check.

Ask AI to mark every claim that needs proof.

Not grammar.
Not better words.
Proof.

Use this prompt:

"Read this answer like a recruiter. List every sentence that makes a claim. For each one, tell me what evidence I need before I can keep it."

Then look at the result.

If it says "strong problem-solving skills", add the problem you solved.
If it says "worked on a team", add your actual role.
If it says "improved performance", add the number or remove the line.
If it says "passionate about AI", replace it with what you built, read, tested, or learned.

The goal is not to make the answer sound bigger.

It is to remove sentences you cannot defend in an interview.

AI is useful when it makes your weak lines visible.

You still have to do the honest editing.

Which line in your resume or application would you fact-check first?
