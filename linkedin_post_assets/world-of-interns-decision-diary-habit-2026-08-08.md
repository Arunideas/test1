## Caption

When an interviewer asks, "Tell me about a project," many students remember the output.

They forget the choices.

But the choice is usually the story.

After any project, internship task, club event, freelance task, or class assignment, save a 4-line decision diary:

1. What decision did I make?
2. What options did I compare?
3. What evidence helped me choose?
4. What changed after the decision?

This takes 3 minutes.

It turns "I worked on the dashboard" into a clearer answer:

"I chose the simpler layout because the main metric was getting missed. After the change, the review was easier to scan."

That is stronger because it shows judgement, not just activity.

Try it at the end of your next work session.

Which decision from this week would be worth saving?
