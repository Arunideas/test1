## Caption

AI can turn messy notes into flashcards.

But the useful part is not the flashcard.

It is the gap it shows you.

Try this before an interview:

1. Paste one page of your own notes.
2. Ask AI for 10 interview-style questions.
3. Answer each question before opening the AI answer.
4. Check the weak answers against your original notes.

Do not start by asking, "Explain everything."

Start by testing what you already understand.

That small difference matters.

If you only read AI answers, you may feel prepared.

If you answer first, you can see where your thinking breaks.

Use AI like a mirror, not a mask.

Which subject would you test this way before an interview?
