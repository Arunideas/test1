## Caption

Most interns wait for feedback.

The stronger habit is to make feedback easy to give.

At the end of the day, write a 4-line proof log:

1. What did I work on?
2. What decision did I take?
3. Where did I get stuck?
4. What is my next step?

This is not a diary.

It is a small evidence trail.

It helps your manager see your thinking, not just your output.

It also protects you from the common first-internship fear:

"I was busy, but I do not know how to explain what I did."

Try sending the proof log once or twice a week.

Keep it short.
Keep it specific.
Keep it honest.

What is one line you would include in your proof log today?
