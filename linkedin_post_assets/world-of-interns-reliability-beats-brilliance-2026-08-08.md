## Caption

In your first internship, reliability gets noticed faster than brilliance.

Not because skill does not matter.

It does.

But most teams are not expecting an intern to solve everything on day one.

They are watching for smaller signals:

Do you show up prepared?

Do you confirm what you understood?

Do you update before someone has to ask?

Do you close the loop when a task is done?

An average task delivered clearly builds more trust than an impressive idea that disappears for three days.

If you want to stand out this week, try this:

1. Repeat the task in your own words
2. Share when you will send the first update
3. Raise blockers early, with context
4. Confirm once the work is complete

This is not about acting busy.

It is about becoming easy to work with.

In the beginning, that is a real advantage.

What is one reliability habit every intern should build early?
