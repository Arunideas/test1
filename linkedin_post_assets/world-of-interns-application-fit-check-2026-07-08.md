## Topic

The application fit check before sending your resume

## Caption

Not every internship deserves your resume.

Before you apply, do a 2-minute fit check.

Ask 4 questions:

1. Can I explain the actual work?
If the role only says "support the team", pause.

2. Do I have one proof point?
A project, assignment, tool, portfolio link, or GitHub repo is enough.

3. Will I learn one useful skill?
The role should give you practice, not just a title.

4. Can I write a specific first line?
If your message can be sent to any company, it is too generic.

This check saves time.
It also makes your application sharper.

Apply to fewer roles.
But make each application easier to trust.

What is one thing you check before applying?
