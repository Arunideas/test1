Post copy:

The future skill many students ignore:

clarifying vague work before starting.

Most internship tasks will not arrive like exam questions.

They may sound like:
"Make this better."
"Check this data."
"Prepare something for Monday."

The mistake is to start fast and guess silently.

A stronger habit is slower for 5 minutes:

1. Restate the task in your own words.
2. List what is unclear.
3. Choose the smallest useful first output.
4. Share your assumptions before going deep.

This does not make you look confused.

It shows you can turn messy instructions into usable work.

That is a real workplace skill.

Before your next project, try writing one line:

"I understand the goal as ____. I am assuming ____. I will send the first version as ____."

What would you add?
