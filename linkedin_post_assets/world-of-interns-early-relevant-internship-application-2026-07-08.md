## Caption

Most students wait until an internship application is perfect.

That delay can quietly cost them the role.

For many openings, the first useful batch of applications gets reviewed before the deadline. A clean, relevant application sent early often beats a perfect version sent after the shortlist is almost full.

A simple rule:

If the role matches 70 percent of your skills, apply within 24 hours.

Then spend the next 20 minutes improving only three things:
1. Make the first resume line match the role.
2. Add one proof-based project bullet.
3. Write a two-line note that explains why this internship fits.

Do not send a careless application.
But do not wait a week for perfect either.

Early plus relevant is usually stronger than late plus over-polished.

What slows you down more: finding the right internship, or feeling your application is not ready yet?
