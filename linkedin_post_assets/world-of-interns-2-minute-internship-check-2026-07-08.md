## Caption

Before you apply to an internship, do this 2-minute check.

A real opportunity usually makes 3 things clear:

1. What you will actually work on  
Not just "assist the team". Look for tools, tasks, projects, or outcomes.

2. How the internship is structured  
Duration, stipend, reporting manager, working mode, and selection process should not feel hidden.

3. Who is accountable for learning  
A mentor, team lead, weekly review, or project owner matters more than a fancy title.

If the post only says "great exposure" and asks you to DM quickly, pause.

Good internships respect a student's time before the first interview.
