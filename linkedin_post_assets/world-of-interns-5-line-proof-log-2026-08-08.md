## Caption

Most students remember their work only after the interview starts.

That is too late.

A simple proof log changes the conversation.

After every project, internship task, or college assignment, write 5 lines:

- What problem you solved
- What you tried first
- What broke
- What changed after the fix
- What you would improve next

This turns a normal project into interview-ready evidence.

It also helps you see your real skill gaps without guessing.

You do not need a perfect portfolio first.
You need proof you can explain your work clearly.

This week, pick one old project and write the proof log for it.

Which project would you start with?
