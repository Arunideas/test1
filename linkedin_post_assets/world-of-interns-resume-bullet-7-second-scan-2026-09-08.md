## Caption

The fastest way to weaken a good resume is to make every bullet look equal.

Recruiters do not read line by line first.
They scan for proof.

A bullet survives that scan when it has three parts:

What you did.
What tool or method you used.
What changed because of it.

Weak:
"Worked on a sales dashboard project."

Stronger:
"Built a sales dashboard in Excel using pivot tables to track weekly revenue across 4 regions."

The second line is not louder.
It is easier to trust.

Before applying today, pick one project bullet and ask:

Can a recruiter understand the action, tool, and result in 7 seconds?

If not, rewrite that one line before sending the resume.

What would you add?
