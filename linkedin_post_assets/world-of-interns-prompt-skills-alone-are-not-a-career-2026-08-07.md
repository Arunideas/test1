## Caption

Prompt skills help.

They are not a career by themselves.

A prompt can draft, summarize, and speed up your first attempt.

But companies still look for the judgement behind the output.

Can you check if the answer is true?

Can you turn it into something useful?

Can you explain the trade-off?

Can you improve it when the tool gives you something average?

That is the real skill gap.

For students, the better habit is simple:

Use AI to get a first version.

Verify it with one trusted source.

Edit it until it sounds like your own thinking.

Save a small proof of what changed because of your decision.

The market will keep changing tools.

Your edge is not knowing one prompt format.

Your edge is judgement, context, and the ability to turn output into work someone can trust.

What would you add to this?
