## Caption

💼 Internship Opportunities

A good internship list is not built by scrolling faster.

It is built by removing weak options early.

Try this before you apply today.

Open any role and ask four simple questions:

1. Is the company easy to verify outside the job post?
2. Does the work match one project or skill you can prove?
3. Is the stipend, duration, and workload believable?
4. Can you write a specific first line for the application?

If the answer is no, do not force it into your list.

One student can send 40 rushed applications and feel busy.

Another can send 10 matched applications with proof, role fit, and a cleaner message.

The second student usually learns more from every reply.

Takeaway: Your shortlist should protect your time before your resume tries to win attention.

Which check should students do first?

[ ] Verify the company  
[ ] Match the skill  
[ ] Check stipend realism  
[ ] Prepare proof project
