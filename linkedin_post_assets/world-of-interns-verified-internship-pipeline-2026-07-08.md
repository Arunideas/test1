## Caption

💼 Internship Opportunities

Students, before you trust any internship post today, check the pipeline behind it.

A real opportunity should pass 4 basic checks:

1. Company identity is consistent across website, LinkedIn, and email domain.
2. Role has clear work, not vague "all-rounder" tasks.
3. Stipend, hours, and duration look realistic.
4. Application link does not ask for money or unnecessary personal documents.

One student recently sent us 18 openings. Only 7 looked worth applying to after these checks.

That is not bad news. It saved 11 weak applications and gave more time to write better answers for the right roles.

Takeaway: Do not just collect internship links. Build a verified shortlist.

What should World of Interns help students verify first?

[ ] Company authenticity  
[ ] Stipend and workload  
[ ] Role-skill match  
[ ] Application safety
