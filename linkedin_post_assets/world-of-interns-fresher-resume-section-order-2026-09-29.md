## Caption

If you are a fresher, your resume does not need to pretend you have experience.

It needs to put your strongest proof first.

Many resumes start with:

"Objective"

Then a long skills list.

Then education.

Then projects hidden near the bottom.

That order makes the recruiter work too hard.

Try this instead:

1. Name, role target, links
2. Education, if it supports the role
3. Projects with your role and result
4. Skills grouped by the work they support
5. Certifications only when they match the role
6. Volunteer or college work that shows delivery

The point is not to fill every section.

The point is to move real evidence higher.

A good fresher resume answers one question quickly:

"Can this student do useful work in this internship?"

Before your next application, look at the top half of your resume.

Does it show proof, or only labels?

What section on your resume deserves to move higher?
