## Caption

Most fresher resumes do not fail because something is missing.
They fail because the useful proof is buried.

A recruiter should not have to cross three sections to understand what you can do.

For a fresher resume, try this order:

1. Name, contact, LinkedIn/GitHub.
2. Target role or one clean headline.
3. Two or three role-relevant projects.
4. Skills backed by those projects.
5. Education basics.
6. Certifications only if they support the role.

Move generic summaries, long course lists, and every club certificate lower.

The first half of page one should answer one question:
"Is there enough proof to keep reading?"

If your strongest project is below hobbies, the order is costing you attention.

What section would you move up on your resume?

-- World of Interns
