A fresher resume should not hide its strongest proof.

If you have no full-time experience, the order matters more than the template.

Try this:

1. Name + clear role target
2. Education, only the useful details
3. Projects with problem, tool, and result
4. Internships, volunteering, freelance work
5. Skills grouped by relevance
6. Certificates only if they support the role

What to avoid:

Putting every course, workshop, and hobby before your projects.

A recruiter is scanning for evidence that you can do the work, not a complete autobiography.

Before you send your resume, ask:

"What will they notice in the first 10 seconds?"

If the answer is not your most relevant proof, reorder it.

What would you move higher on a fresher resume?
