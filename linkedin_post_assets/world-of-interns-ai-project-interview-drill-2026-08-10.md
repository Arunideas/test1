## Caption

AI can help you practise a project interview.

But only if you do not let it answer for you.

Most students describe projects like this:

"I built a dashboard."

A recruiter usually wants more:

What problem did it solve?
Which part did you build?
What broke?
What did you improve?

Try this 10-minute drill:

1. Paste your project summary into AI.
2. Ask it to act like a calm interviewer.
3. Answer every question aloud before reading suggestions.
4. Rewrite one weak answer with proof from your code, data, or user feedback.

The goal is not a perfect script.

The goal is to find where your explanation is unclear.

If you can explain one project with evidence, your resume becomes easier to trust.

Which project would you practise this way first?
