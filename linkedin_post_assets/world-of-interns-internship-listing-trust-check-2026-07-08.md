## Topic

A 2-minute trust check before applying to an internship

## Caption

Before you apply to an internship, do one small trust check.

It can save you from wasting a week on the wrong listing.

Start with the basics.

Does the company have a real website?
Does the LinkedIn page show recent activity?
Does the role explain what you will actually do?
Does the recruiter use an official email or a clear profile?
Does anyone ask for money before selection?

You do not need to investigate forever.

You just need enough proof that the opportunity is real, relevant, and worth your effort.

If the listing is vague, the profile is empty, and the process feels rushed, pause before sending your details.

A good internship should tell you three things:

1. What work you will do.
2. What skills are expected.
3. How selection will happen.

Everything else can be discussed later.

Students often think applying fast is the advantage.

Applying carefully is the better advantage.

What is the first thing you check before applying to an internship?
