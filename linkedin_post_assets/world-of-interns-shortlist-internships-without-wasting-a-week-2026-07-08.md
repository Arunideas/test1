## Caption

Most students do not need more internship links.

They need a faster way to reject the wrong ones.

Before you apply, spend 3 minutes checking four things:

1. Does the description clearly say what work you will do?
2. Can you verify the company through a website, LinkedIn page, or employee profiles?
3. Is the stipend, duration, and location written plainly?
4. Are they avoiding deposits, training fees, or vague "DM for details" messages?

If two of these are missing, pause.

The problem may not be your resume.
It may be the listing.

A smaller, verified shortlist usually beats 50 random applications.

What do you check before applying?
