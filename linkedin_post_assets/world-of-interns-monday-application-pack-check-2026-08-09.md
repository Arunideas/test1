## LinkedIn caption

Before Monday applications, do one quiet check:

Is your application pack ready for one role, or is it still trying to fit every role?

A useful pack does not need to be complicated.

It needs:

1. One role-fit line.
Can someone see the exact internship you are aiming for?

2. Two proof points.
Projects, coursework, volunteering, freelance work, or club work are fine if they show the skill clearly.

3. One short note.
Not a long cover letter. Just why this role, why this company, and what you can contribute.

4. One next edit.
Before applying, change one resume bullet so it matches the role better.

This Sunday reset is not about sending more applications.
It is about making tomorrow's first application less random.

What would you add to the pack?
