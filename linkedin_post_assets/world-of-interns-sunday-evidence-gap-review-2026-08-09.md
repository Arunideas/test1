## Caption

Sunday is a good day to check the gap between effort and evidence.

Many students spend the week applying.
Then Sunday arrives with the same quiet question:
“Why did nothing come back?”

Sometimes the problem is not the number of applications.
It is that the proof is too hard to see.

Before you send Monday applications, pick one role and write four short lines:

1. What the role asks for
2. What my resume shows in the first screen
3. What proof is missing or buried
4. What one line I can fix before applying

Example:

Role asks for: data analysis
Visible proof: Excel listed under skills
Missing proof: no project result
Monday edit: “Cleaned a 500-row survey dataset and built a summary dashboard.”

This small review does two things.

It makes your resume easier to scan.
It also stops you from applying to roles where you have no visible match yet.

A better application week often starts with one honest evidence gap.

What is one proof line you can make clearer before Monday?
