## Caption

One future skill students can build without waiting for a certificate:

Keep a proof log.

Not a long journal.
Not a polished portfolio.
Just a small record of what you tried, what changed, and what you can now explain.

Most students only write:

"I am learning Python."
"I have good communication skills."
"I am interested in AI."

Those lines are too broad for a recruiter, mentor, or internship manager to trust quickly.

A proof log makes the same learning easier to believe.

Example:

- Monday: cleaned one messy CSV file
- Tuesday: wrote down three errors I fixed
- Wednesday: explained the result in five lines
- Thursday: added one screenshot or link
- Friday: noted what I would improve next

After a few weeks, you do not just have "skills".

You have evidence.

Evidence helps you answer interviews better.
It helps you update your resume honestly.
It helps you notice which skills are actually getting stronger.

Start small this week:

Pick one thing you are learning.
Create one note called "proof log".
Add one useful line every day.

What is one skill you could start tracking with evidence instead of claims?
