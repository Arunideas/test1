Topic: Friday Future Skill - keeping a proof-of-work log while learning.

## Caption

A certificate can show that you finished a course.

It does not always show how you think when the task is unclear.

For internship applications, one underrated future skill is keeping a small proof-of-work log.

Not a portfolio website.

Not a long case study.

Just a simple record of:

1. The problem you tried to solve.
2. The decision you made.
3. The evidence you used.
4. What changed after feedback.
5. What you would improve next.

This helps in three places:

Your resume gets sharper because you can write from real work.

Your interview answers feel grounded because you remember the details.

Your confidence becomes quieter because it is based on evidence, not guesswork.

Start this week with one project, assignment, or club task.

After each work session, write three lines:

What did I try?

What did I learn?

What proof did I create?

Future-ready students are not only collecting skills.

They are learning to show their thinking clearly.

What is one piece of work you could start documenting this week?
