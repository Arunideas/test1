Your resume can be strong and still fail before a human reads it.

One common reason: formatting that looks good to you, but breaks the parser.

Before sending your resume, do a 2-minute plain text test:

1. Copy the full resume.
2. Paste it into a plain notes file.
3. Check if headings, dates, company names and bullet points still appear in the right order.
4. If the text becomes scrambled, simplify the layout.

Avoid:
- text boxes
- tables for every section
- icons in place of words
- two-column layouts if you are applying through portals
- important info hidden in headers/footers

A recruiter may read the PDF.
An ATS may read the text layer first.

Make both easy.
