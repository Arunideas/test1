## Caption

Before you apply to an internship, check if the listing is still alive.

A stale listing wastes more than one application.

It can also make you rewrite your resume for a role that was already filled last week.

Try this 2-minute freshness check:

1. Look for a posted date, deadline, or hiring timeline.
2. Check whether the company has shared the same role recently on LinkedIn.
3. Read the comments or replies for "closed", "filled", or "not accepting".
4. If the listing has no date, ask one clear question before spending time on a custom note.

Good internships usually make the next step visible.

Unclear listings are not always fake.

But unclear listings need one more check before you give them your time.

What do you check before applying?
