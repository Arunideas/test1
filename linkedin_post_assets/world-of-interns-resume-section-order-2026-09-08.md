## Caption

If your resume has little work experience, the order matters more than the template.

Most freshers push "Skills" to the top.

Then recruiters see a long list:

Python, SQL, Excel, Communication, Teamwork, Leadership...

But skills without proof are hard to trust.

Try this order instead:

1. Target role line
2. Education
3. Two strongest projects
4. Internship, volunteering, or club work
5. Skills connected to the proof above

The goal is simple.

Show evidence before labels.

If you say SQL, let one project show where you used it.

If you say communication, let one event, report, or team task prove it.

Your resume should not ask the recruiter to believe keywords.

It should help them see the proof quickly.

Which section is taking too much space on your resume right now?
