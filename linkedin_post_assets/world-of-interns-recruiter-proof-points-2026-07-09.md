Topic: Thursday Recruiter Insight - make the first proof point easy to scan.

Image: world-of-interns-recruiter-proof-points-2026-07-09.png

Alt text: A World of Interns checklist card explaining that recruiters scan internship applications for role match, one relevant project, proof of completion, and a clear outcome.

---

Recruiters do not read your internship application like an essay.

They scan for proof.

Usually in this order:

1. Is the role relevant?
2. Is there one project that matches the work?
3. Is there evidence you finished something?
4. Can they understand it in a few seconds?

A good application makes these answers visible.

Instead of:
"I am passionate about learning and ready to work hard."

Try:
"Built a React dashboard that reduced manual reporting for a college club from 2 hours to 20 minutes."

The second line gives context, work, and outcome.

You do not need a perfect resume.

You need one clear reason for the recruiter to keep reading.

Before applying today, open your resume and highlight the first proof point a recruiter will see.

Would it be clear in a 10-second scan?
