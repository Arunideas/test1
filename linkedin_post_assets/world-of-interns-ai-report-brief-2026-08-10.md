## Caption

Most students use AI to shorten a long report.

That is only half the job.

Before an interview, the goal is not to remember every paragraph.

The goal is to find:

1. what the company cares about
2. which numbers matter
3. what changed recently
4. what questions a recruiter may ask

Try this:

Paste the report, article, or company page into AI.

Ask for a one-page interview brief with five sections:

1. Business summary
2. Important numbers
3. Risks or challenges
4. Terms I should understand
5. Three smart questions to ask

Then do one extra step yourself.

Open the original source and verify every number before you say it aloud.

AI can compress reading time.

It should not replace checking.

If you are preparing for an interview this week, try this on one company report today.

Which source do you usually read before an interview?
