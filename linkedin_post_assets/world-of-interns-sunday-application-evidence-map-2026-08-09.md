Topic: Build a small application evidence map before Monday

## Caption

A strong application does not need to explain everything.

It needs to make the right things easy to verify.

Before Monday, try a 10-minute evidence map for one role you care about.

Draw three columns:

1. What the role asks for.
2. Where your resume shows it.
3. Where the proof lives.

The third column is usually where the real improvement appears.

Maybe a project link needs a short README.
Maybe a resume bullet needs an outcome.
Maybe an interview story needs one specific example from practice.

This is a calm way to improve an application without rewriting your whole profile.

One clear proof trail can make ordinary experience easier to trust.

Before you apply this week, ask:

"If someone had 30 seconds, what evidence would they actually see?"
