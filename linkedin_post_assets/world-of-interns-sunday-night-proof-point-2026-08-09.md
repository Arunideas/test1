```text
Before sending more internship applications tomorrow, do one quiet review tonight.

Open this week's work and write three lines:

1. What did I finish that I can show?
2. What did I learn that changed how I work?
3. What proof can I add to my resume, portfolio, or LinkedIn before Monday?

Many applications describe responsibilities.

Stronger applications show evidence.

That evidence can be small:

- a screenshot of a cleaned spreadsheet
- a before/after resume bullet
- a short note on a bug you understood
- one paragraph explaining a task you completed
- a link to a project update

This is not about making the week look bigger.

It is about making the real work easier to see.

If you did even one useful thing this week, turn it into one visible proof point before opening job boards again.

What is one proof point from this week that you can save today?
```
