# World of Interns LinkedIn post - 2026-07-09

Topic: Thursday Recruiter Insight - why recruiters skip long summaries

## Caption

Most recruiters do not skip your resume summary because they are careless.

They skip it because it often asks them to do extra work.

A long summary says:

"I am hardworking, passionate, and a quick learner..."

But the recruiter is trying to answer three faster questions:

1. What role does this student fit?
2. What proof is visible in the first screen?
3. Is there one project, result, or skill that matches the opening?

If your summary does not answer those, it becomes decoration.

Try this instead:

"Final-year commerce student looking for an HR internship. Built a campus onboarding checklist used by 60 students. Comfortable with Excel, coordination, and follow-up."

Not perfect.

But it gives context.

For students:
make the first screen of your resume easy to scan.

For recruiters:
what is the one line you wish more students wrote clearly?
