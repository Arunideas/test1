## Caption

One future skill that makes a student project look more professional:

Debugging with evidence.

Not "I changed a few things and it started working."
Not "I think the issue was somewhere in the API."

A workplace-ready developer can usually explain:

- what failed
- how they reproduced it
- what error or log they checked
- what they changed
- how they confirmed the fix

This matters because real teams do not just need people who can write code.
They need people who can reduce confusion when something breaks.

Try this on one project today:

1. Pick one bug or weak edge case.
2. Write the exact steps to reproduce it.
3. Capture the error message or wrong output.
4. Change one thing, then test again.
5. Add a short note: "Root cause + fix."

That small habit turns debugging into proof of how you think.

Which bug in your project would be worth documenting this week?
