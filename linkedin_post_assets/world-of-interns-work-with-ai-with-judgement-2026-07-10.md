Topic: Friday Future Skill - learning to work alongside AI instead of against it.

## Caption

Most students are told to "learn AI".

But the skill employers will notice is smaller:

Can you work with AI without switching off your own judgement?

AI can summarise, draft, compare options, and clean up messy notes.

It cannot understand your college constraints, your project limits, your interview context, or the details you forgot to mention.

That is where your judgement comes in.

A useful practice:

1. Ask AI for a first draft.
2. Check every claim against your real work.
3. Add the missing context.
4. Rewrite the final answer in your own words.
5. Keep one line that explains your decision.

This turns AI from a shortcut into a thinking partner.

For internships, that matters.

Recruiters do not need students who can paste perfect answers.

They need students who can use tools, question outputs, and still own the work.

This week, try one small task:

Take an old project description, ask AI to improve it, then mark what is true, vague, exaggerated, and missing.

The final version should sound like you on your clearest day.

Where do you still find yourself over-trusting AI outputs?
