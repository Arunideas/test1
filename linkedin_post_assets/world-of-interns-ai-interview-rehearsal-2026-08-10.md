## Caption

Before an internship interview, do not ask AI to write perfect answers.

Ask it to find the gaps in your real answers.

Paste the role description.
Paste one project you actually built.

Then ask:

1. Which 5 questions will test whether this project matches the role?
2. Where does my explanation sound vague?
3. Which answer needs a number, trade-off, or mistake I learned from?
4. What follow-up question would expose shallow understanding?

Then close the chat.

Rewrite the answers yourself.
Say them once out loud.
Cut every line you cannot defend without the screen.

AI is useful here because it makes the practice uncomfortable before the interview does.

Not to sound smarter.
To notice what you still need to understand.

What is one internship interview question you would want to rehearse this way?
