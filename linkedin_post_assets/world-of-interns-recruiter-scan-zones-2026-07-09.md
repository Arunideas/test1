Caption:

Recruiters rarely read your profile from top to bottom.

They scan for proof.

First, they check the role match.
Then they look for one project or internship that proves it.
Then they decide whether the rest is worth reading.

A long summary can hide the signal.

If your first visible line says "hardworking student seeking opportunity", it does not answer the recruiter's first question:
Can this person do the work for this role?

Try this instead.

Use the top third of your resume or LinkedIn profile for:
1. Target role
2. One relevant skill cluster
3. One proof point with outcome
4. One link or project name they can verify

Example:
"Data analyst intern | SQL, Excel, Python | Built a dashboard that reduced weekly reporting time by 30%"

No big claim.
Just easier scanning.

What is the first proof point recruiters see on your profile?
