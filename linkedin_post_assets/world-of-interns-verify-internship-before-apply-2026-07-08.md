## Topic

How to verify an internship before you apply

## Caption

Before you apply, spend 3 minutes verifying the internship.

A real listing usually answers three basic questions:

What will I work on?
Who will review the work?
What happens after the internship?

If the post hides the company name, has no team footprint, asks for a fee, or promises a certificate before explaining the work, pause.

A better check:

1. Search the company page and employee profiles.
2. Compare the role description with their actual work.
3. Ask one polite question about mentor, stipend, and weekly expectations.

This is not overthinking.
It protects your time.

A good internship should make the work clearer, not more mysterious.

What would you check before applying?
