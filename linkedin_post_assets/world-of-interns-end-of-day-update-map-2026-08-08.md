Post copy:

```text
A short end-of-day update can save an intern from silent rework.

Many interns wait until the task is fully done before saying anything.

It feels safer.

But your manager cannot see what is finished, what is stuck, or where you need a decision.

Try this 4-line update before you log off:

1. What I finished today.
2. What I tried but could not complete.
3. What I will do next.
4. One question that would unblock me.

This is not over-communication.

It is a small map.

It helps your manager correct direction while there is still time.

It also teaches you how work moves in a real team: progress, blockers, next step, decision.

If you are starting an internship, send one clear update before you log off today.

What is one line you would include in your daily update?
```
