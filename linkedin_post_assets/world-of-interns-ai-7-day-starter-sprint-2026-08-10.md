# World of Interns LinkedIn post - 2026-08-10

Topic: Monday AI Tool - use AI to turn an internship role into a 7-day starter sprint before joining or applying.

## Caption

Most students use AI after they feel stuck.

Try using it one step earlier.

Before you apply for an internship, paste the role description into AI and ask:

"Turn this internship into a 7-day starter sprint for a beginner. What should I learn, practise, and prepare before I speak to the team?"

Then ask for five sections:

1. Words I should understand
2. Tools I should revise
3. One small practice task
4. Questions to ask a mentor or interviewer
5. Proof I should collect while learning

This is not about pretending you already know everything.

It is about arriving with better questions.

If the role mentions analytics, your sprint may include one dataset, one chart, one insight, and one explanation of how you found it.

If the role mentions marketing, it may include one campaign teardown, one customer note, one content idea, and one metric to track.

Seven quiet days of preparation can make an internship conversation feel less random.

Use AI to make the path visible.
Then do the work yourself.
