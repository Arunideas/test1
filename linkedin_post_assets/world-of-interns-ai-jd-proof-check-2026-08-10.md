# World of Interns LinkedIn post - 2026-08-10

Topic: Monday AI Tool - use AI to turn an internship job description into proof questions before applying.

## Caption

Before you apply to an internship, paste the role description into AI and ask one question:

"What will this company expect me to prove in the first interview?"

Then ask it to return four lists:

1. Skills they may test
2. Projects you should be ready to explain
3. Missing proof in your application
4. Questions you should ask yourself before applying

This is not for copying answers.

It is for finding the gap between "I can apply" and "I can explain why I fit."

If the JD says "data analysis", be ready to show one dataset, one tool, one decision, and one result.

If it says "frontend", be ready to show one screen, one state problem, one bug, and one trade-off.

A stronger application is usually not longer.
It is easier to defend.

Before sending your next internship application, try this check for 5 minutes.

What is one line in a JD that students often ignore until the interview?
