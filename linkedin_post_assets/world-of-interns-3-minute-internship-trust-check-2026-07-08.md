## Topic

3-minute internship trust check before applying

## Caption

Before you send your resume, check the internship in 3 places.

Not because every listing is fake.
Because a weak listing can waste your time silently.

A good internship post should make three things clear:

- what work you will actually do
- who you will report to
- how stipend, duration, and location are handled

Then do a quick trust check.

1. Open the company's LinkedIn page.
Is there recent activity, real employees, and a website that matches the role?

2. Search the company name with "internship" and "review".
Do the results look consistent?

3. Read the application message again.
Any deposit, training fee, vague "selection guarantee", or pressure to decide today is a stop sign.

This takes 3 minutes.
It can save you from 3 weeks of confusion.

What is one check you always do before applying?
