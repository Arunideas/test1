**Date:** 2026-09-29
**Pillar:** Tuesday Resume Review
**Topic:** Replace skill ratings out of 5 with proof recruiters can verify
**Image:** world-of-interns-skill-ratings-resume-fix-2026-09-29.png

## Caption

A 4/5 skill rating does not help a recruiter.

Students often write:

Excel - 4/5  
Python - 3/5  
Communication - 5/5

It feels honest.

But the recruiter still does not know what you can actually do.

Replace ratings with proof:

- Excel: built a sales tracker with pivots and charts
- Python: cleaned 5,000 rows and automated a weekly report
- Communication: presented a project update to a 6-member team

Keep 8-12 skills that match the internship.

Put proof near the skill: project, coursework, internship, competition, lab, or GitHub link.

If there is no proof yet, move that skill lower or leave it out.

A resume is not a confidence meter.

It is a proof map.

Which skill on your resume can you back with evidence today?
