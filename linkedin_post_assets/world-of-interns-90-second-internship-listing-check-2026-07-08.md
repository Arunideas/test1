## Caption

An internship listing can look exciting and still be unclear.

Before sending your resume, do one small check.

Ask: can I explain this role in one sentence?

If the answer is no, read again.

Look for four signals:

1. The work is specific, not just "assist the team".
2. The company has a real website, page, or employee trail.
3. Stipend, duration, location, and mode are written plainly.
4. There is no fee, deposit, or private payment step.

A clear internship may still be competitive.
An unclear internship can waste a week.

Save the roles that pass the check.
Skip the ones that make you guess.

What is one red flag you always check before applying?
