## Caption

A good question in week one is not:

"What should I do?"

It sounds more like:

"I found A and B. I think B fits because __. Am I missing anything?"

Managers do not expect interns to know the system on day one.

They do notice how you handle not knowing.

Before you ask, make a tiny question map:

1. Goal: what am I trying to finish?
2. Tried: what did I already check?
3. Options: what are the two possible paths?
4. Ask: what exact decision do I need from someone?

This does two things.

It saves the other person from guessing.

It shows you are learning the work, not only waiting for instructions.

In your first week, the point is not to ask fewer questions.

It is to ask questions that help the team answer faster.
