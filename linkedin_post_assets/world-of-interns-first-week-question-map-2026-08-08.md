## Caption

Most interns are told:

"Ask questions."

That advice is true.

But it is also incomplete.

Because the question you ask changes the answer you get.

If you ask:

"What should I do?"

You may get a task.

If you ask:

"What does a good version of this look like?"

You get the standard.

If you ask:

"Who will use this after I finish it?"

You get context.

If you ask:

"What mistake should I avoid?"

You get the risk before you step into it.

The first week of an internship is not only about proving yourself.

It is also about learning how work moves inside that team.

A simple question map helps:

1. Outcome - what should this achieve?
2. User - who is this for?
3. Standard - what does good look like?
4. Risk - what should I avoid?

These questions do not make you look confused.

They make your work easier to trust.

And for a first internship, trust is built in small, clear moments.

What is one question every intern should ask in their first week?
