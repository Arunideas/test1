## Topic

Monday AI Tool: using AI to turn a long report, JD, or company document into a practical interview prep brief.

## Caption

Most students use AI to make answers shorter.

A better use before an interview: turn a long company report, JD, or case study into a 10-minute prep brief.

Try this prompt:

"Read this like an interviewer. Give me:
1. the 5 business points I should understand
2. the words this company keeps repeating
3. 3 likely interview questions
4. one example from my resume that connects to each question
5. the gaps I should not pretend to know"

Then check every output against the original document.

Do not copy the AI's sentences into your interview.

Use it to see the map faster, then explain the route in your own words.

The goal is not to sound polished.

The goal is to stop walking into interviews with only surface-level prep.
